Discover Tajikistan

We'll show you around before the wedding — here is a taste of what awaits.

Iskanderkul Lake

A turquoise alpine lake in the Fan Mountains, legendary resting place of Alexander the Great's horse. Crystal-clear waters ringed by dramatic snow-capped peaks.

Hissar Fortress

An ancient fortress over 2,500 years old, standing at the gateway to Dushanbe. Step into Silk Road history and centuries of Tajik civilisation.

Seven Lakes (Haft Kul)

Seven cascading mountain lakes in the Zeravshan range, each shimmering in its own shade of blue and green. A natural wonder that will stay with you forever.

Dushanbe City

Tree-lined boulevards, the magnificent National Museum, Rudaki Park, and the vibrant Green Bazaar bursting with spices, silk, and dried fruits.
